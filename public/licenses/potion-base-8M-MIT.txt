potion-base-8M
Source: https://huggingface.co/minishlab/potion-base-8M
Authors: The Minish Lab. Licence: MIT.

Used in StudyVault AI as an on-device static word-piece embedding for "Match by meaning" in chat.
The vectors are quantised to int8 (scripts/prepare-embedding-model.mjs); the vocabulary is unchanged.
